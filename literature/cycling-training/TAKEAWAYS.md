# Practical cycling-coach takeaways

Prepared 22 October 2026. One corresponding takeaway document is provided for every paper in this collection: the original 15 selections and two additional user-supplied studies. These summaries translate the evidence into coaching decisions for cyclists with varied goals, experience and time budgets. They do not prescribe one weekly schedule.

Each document separates the **paper's findings**, **practical coaching judgment**, **limits on the conclusion**, and **proposed application requirements**. The application requirements are suggestions for later review/rework, not a description of implemented behavior or validation of a new algorithm. Detailed methods, publication inconsistencies and access provenance remain in each paper's research assessment.

Confidence should follow the evidence for the particular claim: a systematic review does not automatically make every recommendation certain, a nonsignificant result does not prove equivalence, and an effective studied workout does not validate an entire training plan. These summaries do not introduce a new formal certainty grading system.

## Using the takeaways together

For planning, start with Galán-Rioja and Rosenblat. For a process that observes and responds to the athlete, read Saw, Manresa-Rocamora and Meeusen. Use the interval papers to choose and dose session options within that process. Read the remaining papers when the goal calls for event preparation, longer-ride durability, strength work, nutrition decisions or a closer look at fitness estimates.

The reviews and their included experiments overlap. For example, the 2020 HRV study contributes to the earlier HRV review: reading both does not create an additional independent replication. Alfonso 2025 is later evidence and should be considered separately, with its substantial attrition and outcome-specific findings retained.

The 2019 Javaloyes summary is explicitly **abstract-only**. The 2020 Javaloyes and 2025 Alfonso papers do not verify its missing full methods. Little and Saw have available full text but no original PDFs. Neal's separate erratum remains unresolved. Individual documents preserve these limits rather than upgrading their certainty.
