# Original-PDF verification

Checked 22 October 2026. Page numbers below are **one-based PDF pages**; printed journal pages are given where helpful. These checks used page images rendered from the saved original PDFs, not just extracted text. The PDFs, full extracted text, and page renders are local research copies under the Git-ignored `sources/` folder. SHA-256 hashes and exact ETH read-offset sequences are recorded in [access-status.json](access-status.json).

All seven PDFs downloaded through ETH MCP were read using `eth_read_paper`, following each `next_offset` through the final `null`, including back matter and references. Supplementary files cited by a paper were not automatically included; in particular, Rosenblat's supplementary polarization-index equations were not retrieved or verified. A complete text-read record is not a claim that every cited source or supplementary analysis was independently reproduced.

| Paper | Original checked | Consequence for interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| [Rosenblat](papers/39888556/notes.md) | PDF pp. 11–12; Figures 3–4; printed pp. 665–666 | Overall intervals cross zero. Figure 4 reports a subgroup interaction p = .04, while competitive and recreational contrasts are individually nonsignificant (p = .13 and .18). This does not establish a preferred model within either subgroup. Figure 3 contains different intention-to-treat and per-protocol estimates; the notes identify which estimates are used. |
| [Llanos-Lagos](papers/40632222/notes.md) | PDF p. 6 / printed p. 198, equations (1)–(5); pp. 18–19 / printed pp. 210–211, Figures 3–4 | The equations use changes and pooled **post-intervention** SD, with a small-sample correction; damaged extraction must not be copied as code. Figure 3 makes negative values beneficial for efficiency; Figure 4 makes positive values beneficial for performance. The abstract's positive efficiency magnitude and the negative figure/result direction must not be treated as the same signed quantity. Standardized effects are not percentage performance changes. |
| [Sánchez-Jiménez](papers/40613880/notes.md) | PDF p. 14 / printed p. 176, work relation and accompanying discussion | Power in watts × seconds gives joules. Divide by 1000 for kJ. With variable power, sum/integrate the power over time. The work metric is mechanical energy, not dietary expenditure; equal kJ need not imply equal physiological fatigue. |
| [Gejl & Nybo](papers/34001184/notes.md) | PDF p. 6, Figure 2 | Confirms pooled SMD 0.17, CI −0.15 to 0.49, p = .29 and the direction of benefit. The estimate does not establish superiority and does not prove exactly zero effect. |
| [Manresa-Rocamora](papers/34639599/notes.md) | PDF p. 8, Table 2; p. 15, Figure 6 | HRV recording and baseline rules differ across studies. Endurance-performance SMD 0.20, CI −0.09 to 0.48, p = .18. Better HRV indices and better performance are different outcomes. |
| [Wang](papers/37163550/notes.md) | PDF p. 8, Figures 3–4 and narrative; p. 9, Table 2 | Controlled TT comparison favors tapering. Between-subgroup p-values for intensity .10, volume .32, frequency .42, and taper type .60 do not establish optimal settings. Duration differs (.02), but the 8–14-day estimate has only 18 participants and substantial uncertainty. The narrative's p < .05 for reduced frequency conflicts with Table 2's p = .16 and interval crossing zero. Printed volume categories overlap at 60%; do not build exact boundaries from that table. |
| [Meeusen](papers/23247672/notes.md) | PDF p. 13 / printed p. 198, Figure 3; p. 14 / printed p. 199, load definitions; p. 16 / printed p. 201, Tables 1–2 | Clinical flowchart/checklist require medical exclusion and expert interpretation. Session-RPE load = duration in minutes × specified session-RPE score; weekly load is summed. Monotony = mean daily load / daily-load SD; strain = weekly load × monotony. Units are arbitrary, and SD = 0 makes monotony undefined. These constructs do not provide validated probabilities of illness/injury. The >5% increase item in the assessment checklist is not a proven safe training-ramp limit. |

## Manual imports: MinerU plus saved originals

Seven further PDFs were supplied locally on 22 October 2026, alongside MinerU conversions. They were assessed from the relevant Methods, Results and coaching discussion, with the following original-page checks. These files were not retrieved through ETH MCP and have no ETH pagination claim. Raw Markdown was retained byte-for-byte. Remote MinerU images were not downloaded; the saved PDFs supply the original visuals.

| Paper | PDF pages checked | Interpretation or conversion issue |
|---|---|---|
| [Galán-Rioja](papers/36640771/notes.md) | 3 / printed 114; 6 / printed 117 | Figure 1 has inconsistent screening labels/counts. Sample includes 161 cyclists, 10 women. Table 2's modified PEDro assessment is not a general high-certainty rating. Listed aggregate intensity percentages do not form a consistent 100% recipe; do not build a plan from those averages. |
| [Seiler](papers/21812820/notes.md) | 3–6 / printed 76–79 | Verified two-minute rests for 4×8/4×4 and three minutes for 4×16. Tables 2–3 and Figures 1–2 distinguish training volume, composite changes and individual endpoints. The 4×8 group contains only men. The PDF body is largely image-based; ordinary text extraction is not usable for the main text. |
| [Rønnestad](papers/24382021/notes.md) | 2–3 / printed 144–145; 5 / printed 147 | Methods and Table 2 specify 3×13×30-second efforts, 15-second within-set rests and 180 seconds between sets. Table 2 gives 900 seconds total recovery and 2070 seconds total block time (34.5 minutes), excluding warm-up/down. Narrative wording calls recovery nine minutes, while Perspectives gives 2.5-minute set rests. These are original-paper inconsistencies. Figure significance markings and the narrative p = .056 for 40-minute improvement also warrant caution. No exact protocol is silently repaired. |
| [Neal](papers/23264537/notes.md) | 3–5 / printed 463–465 | Eleven completers, not twelve; three weekly laboratory sessions and different total volume/load. PPO = final completed-stage power + (seconds in final incomplete stage / 180) × 40 W is specific to that ramp protocol. Table 3 and Figure 2 do not establish superiority for every endpoint; the 40-km performance difference is not significant. The separate erratum is still missing. |
| [Wong](papers/36803419/notes.md) | 4–6 / printed 2580–2582 | Figure 2 shows oxygen-uptake stabilization; Figure 3 reports lactate behavior. Seven participants hit a 40-minute cap at FTP, so approximately 34 minutes is not an uncensored mean exhaustion time. The kinetic equation is typographically ambiguous even in the PDF and is not transcribed into code. MinerU also interleaves some discussion/conclusion text. |
| [Javaloyes 2020](papers/31490431/notes.md) | 2–5 / printed 1512–1515 | This is a different paper from Javaloyes 2019. Methods and Tables 1–2 reverse group n labels. Original TRIMP expression has “between VT2 and VT2,” seconds as time units and no explicit high-zone multiplier; not repaired or adopted. Figure 2 is a study decision schema. Figure 3 shows within-group changes, not proof of between-group superiority. |

Alfonso 2025: original PDF pp. 2–3 and 5–6 were checked. Only 28 of 116 eligible participants completed. Listed dropout reasons sum to 91, versus the stated 88 noncompleters, without clear overlap accounting. The stated WB formula with four 1–7 items implies −20 to +4, whereas the original claims −20 to +10. Table 2 reports within-group tests; Figure 2 only marks the one-minute Group 2 versus Group 1 and five-minute Group 3 versus Group 1 differences. The unusually large reported Cohen's d values were verified as printed, not reproduced. Supplementary A/B and OSF data were not reviewed; no exact decision algorithm was imported. See the [assessment](papers/41028151/notes.md).

Exact file hashes, page renders and provenance are in [access-status.json](access-status.json) and [manual-import.json](manual-import.json). Visual verification identifies what is actually printed; it does not resolve a publication error or substitute for an erratum.
