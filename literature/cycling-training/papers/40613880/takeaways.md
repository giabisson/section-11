# Coach takeaways: Sánchez-Jiménez et al. (2026 issue; online 2025): durability

**Paper:** Is intensity the most important factor in determining the amount of prior work accumulated that affects cyclists' acute durability? A systematic review.

[Study / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00421-025-05885-0) · [Detailed assessment](notes.md) · [All paper takeaways](../../TAKEAWAYS.md)

**Evidence basis:** Full paper assessed; selected original-PDF figures, tables or equations checked. See the detailed assessment for scope and unresolved issues. Prepared 22 October 2026.

## Main coaching lesson

**Consider how an athlete performs after accumulated work when the goal demands it.**

## What the paper adds

The review covered 21 studies and 585 participants, mainly male competitive/professional cyclists. Subsequent performance depended on prior-work intensity, duration, athlete level and the assessment used. Equal mechanical work did not describe an equal fatigue challenge. Fresh capacity and performance after prolonged work were not interchangeable, and critical power could remain stable while other measures declined.

## Coaching application

These are practical coaching interpretations, not a directly tested decision algorithm.

- Describe the target event's duration, terrain and decisive efforts, including whether they occur after substantial riding.
- Where appropriate and feasible, consider existing longer rides and performance later in a ride when evaluating preparation.
- Interpret deteriorating performance with pacing, food intake, conditions and data quality in mind before concluding that fitness has fallen.
- Choose assessments that inform the goal without routinely requiring an exhausting test.

## Limits on the takeaway

These studies mainly examine acute impairment, not the best long-term durability-training program. They do not prove that high intensity replaces event-specific volume or establish a universal kJ threshold for fatigue. Transfer to novices and other underrepresented populations is uncertain. Suggested training approaches remain coaching judgment unless separately tested.

## Implication for a coaching application

Keep the context of a power result: fresh or after prior work, with what duration and intensity. Use load summaries as partial descriptions rather than complete readiness measures. Mechanical work in kJ requires integration of power over time and division by 1000; it is not dietary energy expenditure. These are proposed requirements, not claims about implemented repository behavior.
