# Coach takeaways: Manresa-Rocamora et al. (2021): HRV-guided training synthesis

**Paper:** Heart Rate Variability-Guided Training for Enhancing Cardiac-Vagal Modulation, Aerobic Fitness, and Endurance Performance: A Methodological Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis.

[Study / DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph181910299) · [Detailed assessment](notes.md) · [All paper takeaways](../../TAKEAWAYS.md)

**Evidence basis:** Full paper assessed; selected original-PDF figures, tables or equations checked. See the detailed assessment for scope and unresolved issues. Prepared 22 October 2026.

## Main coaching lesson

**Use HRV consistently and in context, with performance and symptoms as separate observations.**

## What the paper adds

Eight studies included 199 participants of varied training levels and sports, with two cycling studies. HRV-guided training improved some cardiac-vagal measures, but endurance-performance superiority over predefined training was not established: standardized effect 0.20, 95% confidence interval −0.09 to 0.48. Studies used different recording methods, baselines, averaging and decision thresholds.

## Coaching application

These are practical coaching interpretations, not a directly tested decision algorithm.

- If HRV is used, establish the athlete's usual values with a consistent device, position, timing and HRV index.
- Interpret patterns with completed training, performance, symptoms and non-training stress, rather than treating every daily change as decisive.
- Make a missing or unreliable measurement reduce confidence. It should not silently count as a normal observation.
- Review whether collecting HRV adds useful information for this athlete and remains practical.

## Limits on the takeaway

A change in HRV is not the same outcome as a change in cycling performance. No one baseline duration, rolling average or cutoff was shown to be universally optimal. Small studies and methodological/bias concerns limit confidence. Particular mean ±0.5 SD rules are study choices, not population-independent clinical boundaries. This 2021 synthesis does not include the later Alfonso 2025 experiment.

## Implication for a coaching application

Keep raw observations, the exact metric, baseline method and data-quality status available. HRV should inform a traceable coaching decision rather than provide an unexplained binary authorization to train hard. These are proposed requirements, not claims about implemented repository behavior.
