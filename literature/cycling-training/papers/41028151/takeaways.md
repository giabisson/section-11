# Coach takeaways: Alfonso et al. (2025): HRV, resting heart rate and well-being

**Paper:** Individual training prescribed by heart rate variability, heart rate and well-being scores in experienced cyclists.

[Study / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-13540-z) · [Detailed assessment](notes.md) · [All paper takeaways](../../TAKEAWAYS.md)

**Evidence basis:** Full paper assessed; selected original-PDF figures, tables or equations checked. See the detailed assessment for scope and unresolved issues. Prepared 22 October 2026.

## Main coaching lesson

**Combine observations to understand the athlete, while treating exact scoring rules as unvalidated.**

## What the paper adds

This experiment compared HRV-only guidance with HRV plus well-being and with HRV plus well-being plus resting heart rate. Twenty-eight experienced male cyclists completed nine baseline days and 31 intervention days. Between-group comparisons favored HRV plus well-being for one-minute power and the three-marker approach for five-minute power, relative to HRV alone. General superiority for 20-minute power or FTP was not established.

## Coaching application

These are practical coaching interpretations, not a directly tested decision algorithm.

- Use subjective feedback to contextualize physiological observations, especially when the athlete reports fatigue despite a reassuring wearable result.
- Keep sleep, soreness, stress and fatigue available as individual observations so disagreement is visible.
- Assess the effect of monitoring on practical decisions and subsequent training, rather than assuming that adding inputs automatically improves coaching.

## Limits on the takeaway

Only 28 of 116 eligible participants completed (about 76% attrition), creating substantial selection concerns. All groups received guided training, so this is not a comparison with conventional fixed planning. Devices, measurement conditions, training execution and tests varied. More significant within-group results in the three-marker group do not establish the added value of resting heart rate over HRV plus well-being. The printed well-being score range conflicts with its stated formula; reported unusually large effect sizes were not independently reproduced. Supplementary decision rules and participant data were not reviewed.

## Implication for a coaching application

Support multiple observations with clear measurement provenance and missing-data handling. Keep decision logic explainable and validate it separately. This paper does not validate the application's composite readiness weights, cutoffs, injury prediction or multi-month planning. These are proposed requirements, not claims about implemented repository behavior.
