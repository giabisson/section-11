# Coach takeaways: Wong et al. (2022 issue; online 2023): interpreting FTP

**Paper:** Functional threshold power is not a valid marker of the maximal metabolic steady state.

[Study / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2023.2176045) · [Detailed assessment](notes.md) · [All paper takeaways](../../TAKEAWAYS.md)

**Evidence basis:** Full paper assessed; selected original-PDF figures, tables or equations checked. See the detailed assessment for scope and unresolved issues. Prepared 22 October 2026.

## Main coaching lesson

**Keep the meaning and provenance of a fitness estimate attached to its training targets.**

## What the paper adds

Thirteen cyclists exercised at a particular FTP estimate and 15 W above it. Oxygen uptake stabilized at both intensities without reaching peak uptake, challenging the identification of that FTP with the heavy/severe intensity boundary. Seven participants reached the protocol's 40-minute cap at FTP; the reported approximately 34-minute average is therefore not an uncensored mean time to exhaustion.

## Coaching application

These are practical coaching interpretations, not a directly tested decision algorithm.

- Record when FTP was estimated, by which test, on which equipment and in what training context.
- Use power targets as a starting point, then reconcile them with repeatable sustained performance and perceived effort.
- Name the actual denominator when prescribing intensity: FTP, ramp peak power, critical power and physiological thresholds describe different measurements.
- When observations disagree with a stored estimate, investigate the test, equipment and current condition before changing every target.

## Limits on the takeaway

This small study examined a specific 20-minute-derived FTP protocol, with trainer testing and constant-load trials on different equipment. It does not show that FTP has no practical value, define each rider's physiological boundary directly, or validate a universal replacement multiplier. Its findings should not be generalized to every FTP determination method or interpreted as a universal 34-minute FTP duration.

## Implication for a coaching application

Represent fitness estimates with method, date, equipment and uncertainty. Avoid silently treating an Intervals.icu FTP value as a directly measured physiological threshold; handling stale or contradictory estimates is a proposed application safeguard. These are proposed requirements, not claims about implemented repository behavior.
