# Coach takeaways: Meeusen et al. (2013): overtraining consensus

**Paper:** Prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of the overtraining syndrome: joint consensus statement of the European College of Sport Science and the American College of Sports Medicine.

[Study / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0b013e318279a10a) · [Detailed assessment](notes.md) · [All paper takeaways](../../TAKEAWAYS.md)

## Main coaching lesson

**Let persistent underperformance, excessive fatigue and health concerns interrupt routine progression.**

## What the paper adds

This ECSS/ACSM consensus treats maladaptation as a balance of training and recovery affected by multiple stressors. It distinguishes transient fatigue/overreaching from prolonged problems and emphasizes actual performance, individual response, recovery, nutrition, sleep and illness. No single biomarker diagnoses overtraining syndrome; clinical assessment requires considering and excluding other causes.

## Coaching application

These are practical coaching interpretations, not a directly tested decision algorithm.

- Record completed training and how the athlete responds, rather than assuming that prescribed work was done.
- When fatigue is excessive or performance repeatedly declines, reconsider load and ask about recovery, illness, injury, food intake and life stress.
- Allow recovery and individualized return after illness or injury; persistent unexplained problems warrant appropriate clinical assessment.
- Make communication easy so the athlete can report concerns before the next demanding session.

## Limits on the takeaway

This is expert consensus informed by research, not proof of a precise automated algorithm. It is a 2013 statement and does not replace current condition-specific clinical or return-to-sport guidance. Normal HRV or a favorable load score cannot rule out illness or injury. A checklist item mentioning a >5% volume increase is not a validated universal maximum safe weekly ramp. Session-RPE, monotony and strain are monitoring descriptions, not injury probabilities.

## Implication for a coaching application

Use explicit symptom/fatigue checks that can pause automatic progression and trigger follow-up. Maintain uncertainty and escalation paths; a chatbot should not diagnose overtraining syndrome from a composite score. These are proposed requirements, not claims about implemented repository behavior.
