# Coach takeaways: Rosenblat et al. (2025): intensity distribution

**Paper:** Which Training Intensity Distribution Intervention will Produce the Greatest Improvements in Maximal Oxygen Uptake and Time-Trial Performance in Endurance Athletes? A Systematic Review and Network Meta-analysis of Individual Participant Data.

[Study / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-02149-3) · [Detailed assessment](notes.md) · [All paper takeaways](../../TAKEAWAYS.md)

**Evidence basis:** Full paper assessed; selected original-PDF figures, tables or equations checked. See the detailed assessment for scope and unresolved issues. Prepared 22 October 2026.

## Main coaching lesson

**Choose an intensity distribution that fits the athlete, and define how it is measured.**

## What the paper adds

This participant-data synthesis included 13 studies and 348 athletes from several endurance sports. It found no clear overall advantage of polarized over pyramidal training for oxygen uptake or time-trial performance. Training level showed a subgroup interaction, but the competitive and recreational comparisons individually did not establish a winner. Actual heart-rate time in zone reclassified 19% of participants relative to intended distribution.

## Coaching application

These are practical coaching interpretations, not a directly tested decision algorithm.

- Name the zone model and distinguish session purpose, power time in zone and heart-rate time in zone before comparing a plan with a study.
- Use lower-, moderate- and higher-intensity work according to the athlete's goal, training history, total load and recovery.
- Review both goal-specific performance and the athlete's ability to sustain the training. A change in oxygen uptake alone is an incomplete outcome.

## Limits on the takeaway

Failure to detect a difference is not proof that all distributions are equivalent. Evidence spans short interventions, different sports and limited female representation. The subgroup findings do not justify labeling pyramidal training best for every recreational cyclist or polarized training best for every competitive cyclist. An 80/20 split of session types is not the same prescription as 80/20 of recorded minutes.

## Implication for a coaching application

Attach the zone system, measurement method and time window to every intensity-distribution summary. Treat a chosen distribution as a planning hypothesis to review, rather than a pass/fail ratio. These are proposed requirements, not claims about implemented repository behavior.
