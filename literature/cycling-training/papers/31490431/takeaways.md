# Coach takeaways: Javaloyes et al. (2020): HRV versus block periodization

**Paper:** Training Prescription Guided by Heart Rate Variability Vs. Block Periodization in Well-Trained Cyclists.

[Study / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000003337) · [Detailed assessment](notes.md) · [All paper takeaways](../../TAKEAWAYS.md)

**Evidence basis:** Full paper assessed; selected original-PDF figures, tables or equations checked. See the detailed assessment for scope and unresolved issues. Prepared 22 October 2026.

## Main coaching lesson

**Distinguish the potential value of flexible timing from a demonstrated performance advantage.**

## What the paper adds

Twenty trained cyclists were recruited and 15 completed an eight-week comparison after a two-week baseline. HRV-guided and block-periodized groups both trained about 11 hours weekly. Several fitness/performance measures improved within the HRV group, but between-group superiority was not established. The HRV approach adjusted the timing of session types using a rolling log-RMSSD measure.

## Coaching application

These are practical coaching interpretations, not a directly tested decision algorithm.

- Keep some flexibility in when demanding sessions occur, informed by the athlete's recent response and practical constraints.
- If testing an HRV-guided approach, define its measurement method and baseline explicitly, and assess completed training and performance over time.
- Separate an athlete's improvement from a claim that the monitoring method caused more improvement than an alternative plan.

## Limits on the takeaway

This is a distinct study from Javaloyes 2019. Small samples, compliance-related exclusions and a demanding block comparator constrain generalization. The study used standardized morning supine measurements; its thresholds should not be transferred unchanged to overnight wearable values. Methods and tables disagree on group-size labels. The original printed TRIMP equation also contains unresolved zone/weight errors and is not safe to reproduce directly.

## Implication for a coaching application

Offer a transparent adjustment process without advertising this paper as validation of a readiness score. If a recommendation changes, retain the reason and evaluate whether the change helped completion, recovery and goal-specific progress. These are proposed requirements, not claims about implemented repository behavior.
